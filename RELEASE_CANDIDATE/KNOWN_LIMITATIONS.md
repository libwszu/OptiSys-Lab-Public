# Known limitations

- G2 remains `QUALIFIED_ONLY` because its pre-registration deviations were disclosed and were not repaired by Stage 5.
- G4 remains `QUALIFIED`; Stage 5 verifies the assumed P1 geometry, not the real device.
- The source angular distribution, N-BK7 lens prescription and receiving-region sizes are assumptions. Absolute pump power is unknown.
- No vendor lens part/drawing, outer diameter, mount envelope, measured fiber profile, coatings, Fresnel losses, decenter/tilt, P1 assembly tolerance, real crystal size, absorption, thermal response or experiment is included.
- The best registered focus point lies at the lower edge of the frozen focus grid; the exact optimum was not bracketed.
- The equal-aperture candidate is a nominal L2 position trade-off only. The ray bootstrap is not an assembly-level confidence interval.
- Remote CI and GPU acceleration were not tested. A browser video was not delivered.
